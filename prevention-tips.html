Cybersecurity Prevention Tips

🔑 Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords for every account.

📱 Two-Factor Authentication

Enable 2FA for an extra layer of account security.

🔄 Keep Software Updated

Install updates to protect against known vulnerabilities.

📧 Watch for Phishing

Never click suspicious links or attachments.

🛡️ Antivirus Software

Use trusted antivirus software and keep it updated.

💾 Backup Your Files

Regular backups protect your important data from ransomware.
