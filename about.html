About Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, devices, and personal information from cyberattacks. It helps keep sensitive information safe from hackers, malware, ransomware, and unauthorized access.

In today's digital world, cybersecurity is important for everyone. Whether you are using social media, shopping online, banking, or completing schoolwork, practicing good cybersecurity habits helps keep your information secure.

Why is Cybersecurity Important?

🔒 Protects Personal Information

Cybersecurity protects personal information such as passwords, addresses, phone numbers, and private files from cybercriminals.

🛡 Prevents Identity Theft

Strong passwords, secure websites, and good online habits help prevent identity theft and financial fraud.

🏢 Keeps Businesses Secure

Businesses use cybersecurity to protect customer information, financial records, and important company data.

💳 Protects Financial Information

Online banking and shopping are much safer when proper cybersecurity practices are followed.
