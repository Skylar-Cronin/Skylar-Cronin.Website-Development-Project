About Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, devices, and personal information from cyberattacks. It helps keep data safe from hackers, malware, and unauthorized access.

Why is Cybersecurity Important?

  • Protects personal information
  • Prevents identity theft
  • Keeps businesses secure
  • Protects financial information
