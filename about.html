About Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, devices, and personal information from cyberattacks.

Why is Cybersecurity Important?

Protects Personal Information

Helps prevent hackers from stealing your private information.

Prevents Identity Theft

Strong cybersecurity helps protect your identity online.

Keeps Businesses Secure

Organizations use cybersecurity to protect customer data.

Protects Financial Information

Secure banking and shopping rely on cybersecurity.
