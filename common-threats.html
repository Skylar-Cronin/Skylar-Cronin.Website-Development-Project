Common Cybersecurity Threats

🎣 Phishing

Fake emails and websites designed to steal passwords and personal information.

🦠 Malware

Malicious software that can damage your computer or steal sensitive data.

💰 Ransomware

Locks your files until money is paid to cybercriminals.

👤 Identity Theft

Hackers steal your personal information to impersonate you online.

🔓 Weak Passwords

Easy-to-guess passwords make accounts vulnerable to attacks.

🎭 Social Engineering

Attackers manipulate people into revealing confidential information.
